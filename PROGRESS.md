## Unit learning state

```text
⬜ Not started
→ 🟠 Learning
→ 🟡 Practiced
→ 🔵 Recalled
→ 🟣 Demonstrated
→ 🟢 Retained
```

`★ Mastery` is an exceptional separate badge requiring retained evidence, transfer across contexts, teach-back, and recovery from a subtle failure.

### Evidence gates

- **Learning:** Rahul engaged with the mental model and recorded a prediction, question, or misconception.
- **Practiced:** required attempts exist, relevant tests pass, hints are recorded, and reasoning explains the correction.
- **Recalled:** a closed-book reconstruction succeeds after at least one day.
- **Demonstrated:** Rahul solves or repairs an unseen variation, states the invariant, justifies correctness, and explains complexity without a direct solution.
- **Retained:** later successful retrievals normally occur around 7 and 21+ days, or equivalent repeated mock/project evidence exists.
